Why sell with Victoria为何选择 Victoria

Sell smarter. Sell faster. Aim higher.

卖得更聪明、更快、更高。

A great selling result doesn’t happen by accident. It starts with the right strategy.

好的卖房结果不是偶然，而是从正确的策略开始。

After more than 14 years of helping homeowners buy and sell real estate across Toronto and the GTA, I’ve learned one important lesson: a great selling result starts with the right strategy.

在多伦多及大多伦多地区帮助房主买卖房屋 14 年多，我学到了重要的一课：好的卖房结果始于正确的策略。

I’ve seen beautiful homes struggle to sell because they weren’t positioned properly. I’ve also seen how thoughtful improvements, professional staging, effective marketing and skilled negotiation can completely change the way buyers respond to a property. That’s why I developed my 5-V Home SOLD System.

我见过漂亮的房子因为定位不当而难以卖出，也见过用心的改善、专业的 staging、有效的营销和娴熟的谈判如何彻底改变买家的反应。这就是我创立 5-V 卖房系统的原因。

I don’t believe in simply putting your home on MLS® and waiting for buyers. I believe in preparing your home to compete, presenting it to impress, and negotiating to protect your value.

我不相信把房子放上 MLS® 然后坐等买家。我相信要把房子准备好去竞争，用出色的呈现打动买家，并通过谈判守护您的价值。

V

Value Assessment

价值评估

I find the value others might overlook.

发掘别人可能忽略的价值。

Before recommending an asking price, I take the time to understand your property. I look at your home through a buyer’s eyes.

在建议挂牌价之前，我会花时间深入了解您的房产，用买家的眼光审视您的家。

What will make someone choose your property over another? Which features deserve more attention? What could hold buyers back?

什么会让买家选择您的房子而不是别家？哪些特点值得重点展示？什么可能让买家犹豫？

My goal is to identify your home’s strongest selling points and develop a pricing strategy that gives it a competitive advantage.

我的目标是找出您房子最强的卖点，并制定具有竞争优势的定价策略。

I

Intelligent Improvements

明智改善

I help you spend smarter, not more.

帮您花得更聪明，而不是花得更多。

One of the biggest mistakes I see homeowners make is spending money on renovations that may not improve their selling results. I don’t believe every home needs a major renovation before going on the market.

我见过房主最常犯的错误之一，就是把钱花在未必能提升卖房结果的装修上。我不认为每套房子上市前都需要大装修。

Sometimes a fresh coat of paint, better lighting, updated flooring, or a few thoughtful design changes can make a remarkable difference. With my background in professional home staging and my trusted network of contractors, I help you decide what is worth improving and what isn’t.

有时一层新漆、更好的灯光、更新地板或几处用心的设计调整，就能带来显著差别。凭借专业 staging 背景和值得信赖的装修团队，我帮您判断哪些值得改善、哪些不必。

I personally pay attention to the details, from paint colours and lighting to furniture placement and overall presentation.

从油漆颜色、灯光到家具摆放和整体呈现，每个细节我都亲自把关。

My goal is simple: make every improvement count.

我的目标很简单：让每一分改善都物有所值。

C

Compelling Presentation

出众呈现

I make buyers see your home at its best.

让买家看到房子最好的一面。

I believe buyers don’t just purchase square footage. They buy the feeling of being home. That’s why staging and visual presentation are such important parts of my selling strategy.

我相信买家买的不只是面积，而是“家”的感觉。因此，staging 和视觉呈现是我卖房策略中非常重要的部分。

I work with my professional photographer and often plan the details of how a property should be photographed and filmed. I want buyers to feel something when they see your listing.

我与专业摄影师合作，经常亲自规划房子的拍摄和录像细节。我希望买家看到您的房源时能心动。

When your home stands out, it has a better opportunity to attract serious buyers.

当您的房子脱颖而出，就更有机会吸引认真的买家。

T

Targeted Marketing

精准营销

I don’t just want more views. I want the right buyers.

我要的不只是更多浏览，而是对的买家。

Getting your home noticed is important. Getting it noticed by the right buyers is even more important. I develop a marketing strategy for each property.

让房子被看到很重要，让对的买家看到更重要。我会为每套房产制定专属营销策略。

I also pay close attention to how buyers respond once your home is on the market.

房子上市后，我也会密切关注买家的反应。

I monitor the market, listen to feedback, and adjust our strategy when needed.

我持续关注市场、倾听反馈，并在需要时调整策略。

O

Offer Optimization

报价优化

I don’t just bring you offers. I negotiate for your best interests.

我不只是为您带来 offer，更为您的最大利益谈判。

This is where experience really matters. As a Master Certified Negotiation Expert (MCNE), I protect your interests at every step of the negotiation.

这正是经验最重要的时候。作为认证谈判专家（MCNE），我在谈判的每一步守护您的利益。

Protect your interests and work toward the strongest achievable price and terms.

守护您的利益，争取最好的价格与条款。
