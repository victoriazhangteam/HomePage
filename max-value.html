How to max your home value如何让房子卖高价

Spend smarter, not more.

花得更聪明，而不是更多。

You don’t need a major renovation to sell well. You need the right improvements, done right, and presented beautifully.

卖个好价钱不一定需要大装修，而是需要做对的改善、做得到位、呈现得漂亮。

Preparation that pays有回报的准备

Six ways I add value before you list挂牌前，我为您增值的六种方式

Price from the buyer’s side

从买家角度定价

I start by asking what will make a buyer choose your home over the others, then set a pricing strategy that gives it a competitive edge.

先问：什么会让买家选择您的房子而不是别家？再据此制定有竞争优势的定价策略。

Compare before you renovate

装修前先算账

Before you spend, I show you what the home could sell for with and without improvements, so you invest only where it pays back.

动工前，我会为您分析装修与不装修分别可能卖到什么价格，让您只在有回报的地方投入。

Paint, light, floors

油漆、灯光、地板

Often the biggest gains come from the simplest updates: fresh paint, brighter lighting and updated flooring.

最大的提升往往来自最简单的更新：新漆、更明亮的灯光、更新的地板。

Stage for feeling

为“家的感觉”而布置

Buyers don’t just buy square footage. Furniture placement and styling help them picture living there.

买家买的不只是面积。家具摆放和软装帮助他们想象在这里生活的样子。

Plan the photos

规划拍摄

I plan with my photographer how each room should be shot and filmed, so your listing makes buyers feel something.

我与摄影师一起规划每个房间的拍摄与录像，让您的房源打动买家。

One team, start to finish

一站式统筹

I coordinate trusted contractors, stagers and photographers, so preparation stays on time and on budget.

我统筹信赖的装修师傅、布置师和摄影师，确保准备工作按时、不超预算。
